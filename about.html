About

Rough Draft Oy is an independent iOS studio based in Helsinki, Finland. It is based on a simple idea: Done is better than perfect. Focus on shipping simple, and value-additive experiences to users.

Here to ship tools that quietly fit into daily life of users. Rough Draft Oy is operated as a solo practice, valuing clarity, restraint, and long-term care over rapid expansion.
